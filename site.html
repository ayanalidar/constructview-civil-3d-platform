CORE CAPABILITIES

Everything Your Team Needs in One Platform

No more juggling 5 different tools. ConstructView handles viewing, analysis, collaboration, and client presentation.

Real-Time 3D Viewer

Upload IFC, RVT, DWG, SKP, GLB, OBJ, FBX — view instantly in WebGL. Walk from outside façade to interior rooms in one seamless motion. No software install needed.

  • Orbit, pan, zoom, first-person walk
  • Wireframe & X-ray transparency modes
  • Real-time shadows & ambient occlusion

Section Cuts & Analysis

Slice through any axis to inspect internal structure, MEP routing, and rebar placement. Live clipping planes update instantly as you adjust the cut position.

  • X, Y, Z axis cuts with slider control
  • Visual cut plane indicators
  • Clear/restore with one click

Clash Detection

Automatically find conflicts between disciplines. MEP pipe running through a structural beam? Electrical tray colliding with HVAC? Flagged instantly with severity grading.

  • Cross-discipline box intersection
  • Critical/Warning/Minor severity levels
  • Pulsing visual markers at clash points

On-Model Measurement

Click any two points directly on the 3D surface. Get real-time distance with a dashed preview line, then a permanent labeled measurement. No separate tool needed.

  • Point-to-point distance in meters
  • Live preview while hovering
  • Floating labels persist in scene

White-Label Branding

Every client gets their own branded experience. Swap logo, colors, domain, and role names from a simple admin panel. The platform looks like it was built just for them.

  • 8 preset color themes + custom hex
  • Custom domain & logo upload
  • Watermarked exports with client brand

Role-Based Access

Project Manager, Designer, Site Supervisor, Client — each sees exactly what they need. Set permissions per role: view-only, annotate, edit, or full admin.

  • 5 predefined roles + custom
  • Per-project access control
  • Client download restrictions
